Core-first exercise review

Review proposal only. No exercise is enabled, approved or prescribed by this page. Current eligibility and user settings are unchanged.

What the catalogue can support

  • Only one aerobic movement; genuine same-purpose aerobic substitutions are unavailable.
  • Only calf raise and bridge are selectable strengthening candidates; no selectable upper-body strengthening or dedicated higher-load abdominal pattern.
  • Gentle activation and mobility supply most core variety. Repeating them does not make a balanced strengthening programme.
  • The proposed shoulder-constrained profile removes the overhead reach from suggestions. Existing joint/recovery caps may make longer or repeated sessions infeasible.
  • The two Pilates additions remain review records only, absent from the live catalogue and planner.

Bent-knee fall-out

Trunk/pelvic control while one bent knee moves sideways. Do not assume substantial strengthening without reviewing effort and dose.

Purpose: classification pending · Position: floor · Art: missing

Movement and loading details

Supine with feet resting; move one bent knee a small controlled distance outward and return while the pelvis and other knee stay still. Arms remain resting.

Areas involved: hips, knees, back

Hip rotation, bent-knee tolerance, back control and floor/shoulder support require individual review. No treatment dose or range is prescribed here.

Continuation leaflet for people who completed physiotherapy Pilates. It is not clearance for a new programme.

Abdominal/pelvic control with an unsupported leg; potentially more demanding than heel slides. Classification and dose remain unresolved.

Purpose: classification pending · Position: floor · Art: missing

Movement and loading details
