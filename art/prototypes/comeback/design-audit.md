# Structural art review

The user approved the direction, not finished character construction. Address the design before adding pixel ornament.

| Area | Structural issue in the first proof | Correction in this pass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing anatomy | Head is too large relative to the body; short exposed legs, oversized footwear and straight arms make the adult silhouette inconsistent. | Smaller adult head, clearer neck/shoulder connection, revised torso/leg balance, articulated elbows/wrists, slimmer trainers. |
| Development | Shirt marks substitute for physical form in some regions. | Broader shoulder/upper-arm silhouette and restrained thigh/calf changes, preserving skull, height and foot registration. |
| Floor anatomy | Oversized angular face, compressed arm, triangular bent leg and unclear second-foot contact. | Rebuild the profile/neck, separate forearm and hand, contour knee/shin and keep the sliding heel grounded. Still provisional. |
| Lighting | Body is lit from the left but the room window is at the right. The broad wall wedge under the sill looks detached. | One upper-right light direction, uninterrupted wall under the sill and floor-only window light preserving floorboard texture. |
| Room construction | Uniform floor stripes and flat shelf/window details reduce spatial clarity. | Depth-spaced boards, registered seams, shallow architectural recess/shadows and clearer plant/pot volumes. |
| Composition | Workout renders a new mat over a room already containing one, and shows an arbitrary window/plant crop. | Export an independent architecture/light backdrop for the workout; compose one mat and the actual pose over it. |
| Editability | Pixel layers exist but registration and material assumptions need clearer contracts. | Preserve pose parts and document light/camera variants. The far arm is largely occluded in profile; do not add a disconnected limb for completeness. |

The flattened generated reference is concept provenance only. These corrections use native Aseprite pixel layers, not new generation, image warping or higher-resolution paint reduced to sprites. V1 masters/PNGs and two phone captures are retained beside this review for comparison.

## Assessment after the structural pass

Direction accepted; drawing is still provisional. Overall proportions and pose anatomy take priority over small details. The visible corrections are native-pixel revisions, not clinical validation. The source remains a layered pose atlas rather than a deformable anatomical rig. New intermediate stages and walking should wait for a convincing canonical model.
